Heart-ROI cropping: pipeline simulation

01 — why crop at all

Two ways the model can search a scan

Traditional segmentation scans the entire thorax for calcium. ROI cropping restricts the search to a small region around the heart, computed individually for each patient.

Traditional — searches the entire volume

Every voxel in the thorax is a candidate. Ribs, spine, and lung tissue can all be misread as calcium.

02 — how the crop is computed

The box is measured, never fixed

Each patient's crop box comes from their own heart segmentation, expanded by a physical margin. Try the presets and the margin slider — a margin that's too small can clip real calcium near the heart boundary.

03 — training pipeline

What changes when training with ROI cropping

Two extra steps happen before training starts: find the box, then crop the image and its label to that box. Everything after — patch sampling, the training loop — is unchanged.
