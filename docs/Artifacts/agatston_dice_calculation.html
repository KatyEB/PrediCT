Each slice contributes independently to the overall risk matrix. Crucially, the Density Factor depends strictly on the Peak HU (Maximum Intensity) of the deposit on that specific slice—not the average HU. A single very bright pixel elevates the multiplier for that entire local slice deposit. Slices with zero detected calcium simply add 0 to the running total.

Formula: Dice = 2 × Overlap / (Predicted + Ground Truth). Computed natively across the FULL 3D volume simultaneously—not averaged per slice.

Dice measures spatial shape accuracy: Did the model localize the correct pixels? A value of 1.0 indicates a perfect geometric match, while 0.0 means complete failure to overlap. Slices 1–31 and 38–57 (where both prediction and GT are blank/zero) do not artificially inflate the Dice score; only spatial locations with active targets drive this metric.

Agatston measures clinical risk burden: Total volume mass combined with structural radiological density. These two goals are distinct; a model can achieve a beautiful Dice overlap score but calculate an incorrect clinical risk categorization if it under/over-segments highly dense Hounsfield regions.
