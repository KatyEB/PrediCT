HOW TO READ THIS Surfaces are drawn from the predicted probability volume. The stepping you can see is 3 mm slice spacing against 0.37 mm pixels — the real resolution of the scan, not a rendering artefact. Nothing here is smoothed except the heart shell, which is context only. Drag to rotate, scroll to zoom, click a surface to select its lesion and jump the 2D viewer to it.
NOT COMPUTED Calcium is not attributed to a named vessel (LM / LAD / LCX / RCA). That requires coronary centreline extraction or atlas registration, neither of which is implemented in this pipeline. Totals and per-lesion detail are reported; vessel-level scores are not.
