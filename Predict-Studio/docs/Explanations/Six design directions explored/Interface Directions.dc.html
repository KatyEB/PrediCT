PrediCT Studio — interface directions six theses · 2026-08-13

Six things this interface could believe it is for.

Each direction below starts from a different answer to one question: what is the person in front of the screen actually doing? The palette follows from that answer rather than the reverse. All six carry the same five non-negotiables, listed at the foot of each block — model identity visible without interaction, exclusions shown as exclusions, soft prediction values rendered soft, one screenshot sufficient to reconstruct the number, keyboard operable at 1440×900.

01 The Argument 02 The Instrument 03 The Contact Sheet 04 The Ledger 05 The Field 06 The Diff
01

The Argument

The user is not looking at a scan — they are deciding whether to accept a claim, so the screen is shaped like the claim and its warrant.

One column, read top to bottom, in the order a skeptic asks questions: what do you claim → on what grounds → show me → what did you throw away → what produced this. Images appear as evidence exhibits, sized and captioned, not as a workspace. There is no viewport to fly around in; the page is finite and ends.

Remembered by
The word because set as a section rule, directly under the total. The interface commits to a reason out loud.
Sacrifices
Exploration. Eight exhibits are chosen for you; browsing all 120 slices is a secondary view. Repeat daily use will feel slow — this is a thing you read, not operate.
The risk
Prose in a medical tool. Justified because the audience is three readers deciding trust, and a sentence can carry a warrant that a widget cannot.

02

The Instrument

The user is operating a machine over a fixed 120-position axis, and the most interesting fact in the data is which eight positions fired.

The whole volume is permanently on screen as a 120-cell ribbon — one cell per slice, always the same width, never scrolled. Sparsity becomes the primary graphic: 112 empty cells and 8 loaded ones. You do not navigate to find calcium; you see the distribution and jump to it. Everything is driven from the keyboard, and the ribbon is the cursor.

Remembered by
The ribbon. A 1440-wide bar where 112 cells are dark and 8 are amber — the sparsity of the study readable in one glance, from across the room.
Sacrifices
Anatomical context and the lesion table as a table. You see one slice at a time and a per-slice readout; comparing lesion 4 against lesion 17 means a second view.
The risk
Spending a full-width band on 120 mostly-empty cells. Justified because emptiness is the finding: a reviewer should feel how little of the volume the score rests on.
provenance: title bar, single line, never collapses · exclusions: half-height ribbon cells + always-visible count · soft values: three-tier voxel legend with counts, per slice · keys: full set on the status bar
03

The Contact Sheet

The user is inspecting a specimen, and the honest unit of inspection is the whole volume at once, not a slice at a time.

All 120 slices print as a photographic contact sheet, 12 across, every frame identically windowed and captioned with its index. The eight frames with calcium carry registration marks in the margin. Scrolling is replaced by looking. The model's output is not a layer you toggle — it is a red crop mark drawn on the sheet, the way a picture editor marks a frame.

Light or dark, and why
Light sheet, dark frames. Every frame is a #101214 tile with a hard edge, so each slice keeps its own dark surround at exactly the size where greyscale judgement is possible; the sheet between them is paper. The trap of a light medical UI is grey pixels on a light field — here no pixel ever touches the paper.

Eight red-marked frames in a field of 120 grey ones. The sparsity is a photograph of itself, and it prints.
Sacrifices
Detail. At 12-across, a frame is roughly 90 px and a calcium speck is two pixels — you trust the marks and enlarge to verify. Also gives up the smooth scrub through z.
The risk
Treating a diagnostic volume as photographic contact strips, with editor's crop marks standing in for a segmentation mask. Justified because the sheet answers the reviewer's first question — where in the body is this score coming from — without a single interaction.

04

The Ledger

The user is auditing arithmetic: 1064.2 is a sum, and the interface exists so that every term in it can be checked and footed by hand.

Borrowed from double-entry bookkeeping. The lesion table is not a sidebar — it is the screen. Every row shows its own multiplication (area × weight = score) rather than a finished figure, a running balance climbs down the right edge, and excluded candidates get a parallel column that foots to its own subtotal, so nothing leaves the books. Images shrink to 64 px stamps in the left gutter: proof that a row corresponds to a picture, not a place to look at anatomy.

Light, and the greyscale constraint is dismissed on purpose: this direction admits it is bad at looking at images and does not pretend otherwise. The 64 px stamps are identification thumbnails, drawn on their own black tiles with a fixed window; any real inspection hands off to the enlargement view or to direction 02.

Remembered by
The contra section, and the line that says what the withheld rows would have added. The exclusion is quantified instead of merely disclosed.
Sacrifices
The image, almost entirely. Nobody will catch a segmentation error here. It also spends two rows per lesion to show the multiplication, so a 24-lesion study scrolls.
The risk
Accounting-form clichés — rule blue, red footings, banded rows — in a deep-learning tool. Justified because the reviewer's real objection is arithmetic, and accountancy has a 500-year-old grammar for showing that a total is honest.

05

The Field

The protagonist is the uncertainty, not the number: the user is asking how much of this score is a decision the model made and how much is a decision the threshold made.

There is no single score in the hero position. There is a short spine of scores — the total at τ 0.30, 0.40, 0.50, 0.60, 0.70 — with the tier each one lands in, and the current setting marked. The volume appears once, as a fused coronal projection of the probability field: a column of soft light, not 120 slices. Slices exist, but as a drill-down. Probability is rendered as luminance and grain, never as an outline, because an outline is a claim of certainty the model did not make.

Density: sparse and centred. One graphic, one spine, one provenance strip. Numbers are large but never alone — every figure carries the condition it holds under.
Light or dark, and why
Neither: a mid-grey ground at roughly 45 % luminance. On black, a 0.35 voxel reads as faintly present; on white it reads as faintly absent. Against mid-grey, soft values can move in both directions and a partial value looks partial. This is the direction where the greyscale question is answered by refusing both defaults.

The Diff

The unit of study is not one run but a pair, because the actual research question is which labelling strategy produced the difference.

Two mirrored columns, strategy A and strategy B, with a narrow disagreement gutter down the middle carrying the delta for every lesion and every slice. Agreement is drawn quietly; disagreement is the only thing with contrast. A tier flip anywhere in the pair is escalated to the header, because a flip is the only difference that changes a decision. Single-run mode exists and looks like a diff with an empty right column, which is honest about what this tool is for.

Remembered by
The header verdict: NO TIER FLIP. The screen leads with the only comparison that would change what anyone does.
Sacrifices
Room. Two of everything at 1440 px means both images are small and both ledgers are abbreviated, and agreements collapse by default — you have to ask to see what matched.
The risk
Collapsing agreement, which hides most of the data by default. Justified because the paper's finding lives entirely in the disagreements, and every collapsed row is one keystroke from visible.
provenance: three-line pair header split into shared and differing settings · exclusions: kept as rows, with ADMITTED / NEW markers when a strategy changes their status · soft values: rendered soft in both panels; B's soft edge is called out in words · keys: ⇥ / a / x / e

How these divide up

Protagonist. The reasoning chain (01) · the volume as a fixed axis (02) · the image population (03) · the arithmetic (04) · the uncertainty (05) · the delta between strategies (06).
Form of the volume. Chosen exhibits (01) · a 120-cell index (02) · a printed sheet (03) · identification stamps (04) · one fused projection (05) · two matched tiles (06).
Ground. Paper with dark wells (01, 03, 06) · warm graphite separated from the image black (02) · paper that admits it is not for looking (04) · mid-grey so partial values read partial (05).

Two are combinable without losing their thesis: 02 as the working instrument with 01 as its export view, or 05's threshold sweep as a band inside 04's footing. 03 and 06 are whole-screen commitments and do not merge with anything.
